## New Scales!
Some time back I made some work-branded versions of the scale I have on the [Resources](/resources.html) page. Printed on heavy matte paper, with lamination so they would last longer. They have not, however, lasted forever. In addition, due in part to the small size of many things we photograph, the scale was often getting cut off so that only the top row showed up... leaving out fiducials and color references. So I redesigned it with a more fractal design, replicating elements:

</div>

This is my branded version. There is a clean, public domain version on [Resources](/resources.html), with my logos replaced by additional fiducials. You can fit four rows by three columns on a 8.5 × 11-in sheet with 1/4-in margins. I'm going to try printing these tomorrow, and figure out if my local print shop can handle those margins.
